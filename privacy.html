This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Yarn Master (“Game”), operated by Wuhan Changxiang Wonderful Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us”, “our”).

By downloading, installing and playing the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you disagree with this Policy, please uninstall and stop using the Game immediately.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy only applies to the Game. Third-party SDKs for advertising and analytics are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information We Collect

  • Device information: device model, OS version, language, screen resolution, and game version.
  • Game activity data: level progress, in-game actions, ad impressions, ad clicks, rewarded video records, crash logs, and performance metrics.
  • IP address: used for ad targeting and anti-fraud.
  • IDFA: We collect IDFA only after you grant tracking permission via the ATT prompt on iOS 14.5 or later. IDFA is used for ad serving, attribution, and ad optimization. You can disable tracking permission in device settings at any time.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email, camera, microphone, or precise location, unless you voluntarily send us a support email. Game saves are stored locally on your device.

  • Prevent cheating and fraud;

4. Third-Party SDKs

We integrate third-party SDKs for ads and analytics. Their data processing follows their own privacy policies:

  • AdMob (Google): ad serving and attribution — Privacy Policy

  • Firebase: crash reporting and analytics — Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be disclosed when legally required by competent authorities.

5. Data Retention

Anonymous logs and ad statistics are retained for 12 months and then deleted. Local game saves remain only on your device.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, deletion, or correction of your personal data by email at jason@newminigame.fun. You can revoke tracking permission in your device settings.
